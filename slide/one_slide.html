Problem. VANI talks to every seller in one voice and one style for the whole call. A rushed, irritated or confused seller still gets the same pitch, and meetings are lost.
Solution. A persona is generated for each seller from their own data and past calls: voice, language, tone and plan, each with a reason and evidence. VANI adapts it live on five signals and logs every switch.

2 · Persona API FastAPI, Pydantic

Persona engine (rules, deterministic)
Voice · language · tone · plan (opening, personalisation, objection playbook, escalation, guardrails). Each has a reason, data fields, evidence and confidence; missing fields lower the confidence.
Facts layer: package suggestion by turnover (IndiaMART listed prices only); numbers rendered in words by code; greeting built per seller.
Optional LLM: gpt-4.1-mini or claude-haiku-5-5, structured output, injection guard, falls back to rules. Off in this demo.

Business impact

  • More meetings: each seller gets the opening, pace and objection handling that past calls show works for sellers like them.
  • Fewer burnt sellers: fatigued sellers are closed at the first sign of irritation; do-not-call sellers are never dialled (1,178 blocked).
  • Trust and compliance: the same listed prices, spoken in words, for every seller; no invented offers; truthful if asked about automation.
  • Measurable: every switch and outcome is logged per call_ref, so the persona rules can be tuned on real results.
  • Proven: all 5 required signals seen live · 5 clearly distinct sample personas · 106 automated tests · no LLM needed: rules explain every choice.
